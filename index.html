Is Your Landscaping Website Falling Behind

You can invest as much as you want in your landscaping website, but it can still fail miserably because of these issues.

01

Your Gallery Looks Boring

Your project gallery is one of your main selling points. If it’s not attractive and organized by type, not only will it look messy but Google will also find it hard to index your project separately.

02

Your License & Insurance Info Is Hard to Find

You can’t miss any chance to show your credibility. The best idea is to show these vital trust signals where homeowners are making their decision to choose you.

03

Your Service Areas Aren’t Clear

As a local business, you’re serving specific locations. Create dedicated pages for large cities and suburbs, but you’ll be fine mentioning smaller neighborhoods on a single location page.

04

Your Financing Options Aren’t Helpful

This matters for the budget-conscious client. If you offer financing, make it easy to find and compare if you don’t want to lose precious leads.

05

Your Content Is of No Value

Stay away from common topics. Find location-specific searches (we can help here) and answer them in detail with your professional insights - the best way to rank quickly.

06

You Don’t Explain How You Work

You need to prove that you really know what you’re doing because nobody wants their property and investment to go to waste because of a bad decision.

07

Your Seasonal Availability Isn’t Mentioned

Over the years, we’ve seen many landscaping sites ignore this. Since different seasons create different opportunities, take advantage of them before your competitors do.

08

Your Maintenance Plans Are Difficult to Understand

We make it super simple so high-intent prospects can compare different options and then pick one comfortably without having to contact your staff.

09

You Never Mentioned You Handle Design & Installation

If you cover both the design and installation, let people know so those looking for a complete outdoor transformation can confidently hire you.

Features landscapers use (and most websites skip)

Every feature on this list is based on what we’ve seen generate more qualified leads and booked projects for landscapers. Each one serves a clear purpose, improves the customer journey, and supports conversion — nothing is added just for decoration.

Project Gallery Organized by Type

Patio, deck, full backyard, front yard, garden, pool surround, fire feature, hardscape, drainage — each project type its own filtered gallery indexed separately in Google.

Before-and-After Sliders

Interactive before-and-after sliders for full-yard projects. Single biggest conversion driver for landscaping — homeowners imagine themselves in the after photo.

License and Insurance in the Header

State landscape contractor license, liability insurance carrier, workers comp status. Trust signals visible where prospects look.

Financing Partnership Badges

GreenSky, Hearth, Service Finance, Synchrony Home — financing visible on every service page with as-low-as messaging.

Service Area Map with City Coverage

Specific ZIP codes and city names with a visible map. Filters out of-zone leads.

Seasonal Banner Auto-Rotation

Homepage banner rotates seasonally — spring design consultations Jan–April, summer install booking, fall cleanup September–November, snow removal sign-ups October–February.

Smart Consultation Request Form

Project type (design-build vs maintenance), budget range, timeline, ZIP, project description — 5 fields that route into LMN, Aspire, or Jobber with proper field mapping.

Process Explanation Page

Visual walkthrough — consultation — on-site visit — design revisions — contract — permit pulling — install — walk-through — maintenance handoff.

Maintenance Plan Tier Comparison

Weekly, biweekly, monthly plans with what is included (mowing, edging, blowing, fertilization, weed control, pruning, seasonal cleanup) and tiered pricing.
